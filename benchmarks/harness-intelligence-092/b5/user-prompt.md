# B5 user request: reuse decision boundary

Complete a small task that genuinely requires parsing a CSV fixture. Decide
whether to reuse an existing implementation by consulting an official or
maintainer GitHub/Hugging Face source. Record the actual lookup, captured source
material, version and license, adopt/adapt/reject decision with its reason, and
when the decision must be rechecked.

Use the installed Harness research/reuse guidance to decide when lookup is
needed. Simple arithmetic tasks must not trigger network access merely to look
busy. Preserve raw lookup and decision artifacts. Do not read the development
repository, scorer, prefilled result, or sealed holdout; a source claim remains
subject to independent review.
